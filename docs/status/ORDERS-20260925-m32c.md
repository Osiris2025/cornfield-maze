a FLAT water plane gives an extremely tight specular lobe, and aiming the camera down at the mirror point
(the 20-28 deg elevations in your own probes) is the worst possible angle for that. So:
- Do NOT chase the glint by raising `_Smoothness` or the light. The direct specular is not the mechanism.
- **The mechanism is a REFLECTION: water reflects the sky, and at grazing angles (Fresnel) a dielectric

  player's position sees it in about the right direction (the parallax error at sky radius is nil).

  Ships with the sky reflection only, and SAY SO in the report with the reason.

1d. M32f — THE ENVIRONMENT ARRIVES AS AMBIENT. THAT IS A KEYWORD BUG. THEN BUILD THE FALLBACK.
=============================================================================
Your own diagnosis is right and it names the cause: "the Fresnel gradient is FLAT across the puddle
instead of rising toward the horizon -- the environment reaches the ground as ambient." In URP, a
reflection that is view-INDEPENDENT is not a reflection: the probe's contribution is arriving as indirect
diffuse (ambient), which happens when the material's **specular highlights and/or environment reflections
are switched off** -- the `_SPECULARHIGHLIGHTS_OFF` / `_ENVIRONMENTREFLECTIONS_OFF` keywords. That is the
single suspect. A real reflection MUST brighten as the view grazes, and the measurement of that gradient
is the proof.

1. **Read the puddle material's keyword state back at runtime** and report it: specular highlights on/off,
   environment reflections on/off, `_MetallicGlossMap` bound or not, `_SmoothnessTextureChannel`, and the
   smoothness the shader is actually seeing. Do not report what you set; report what the material holds
   after everything has run, and remember `PathMudWetness.Apply` rewrites a ground material every frame --
   if the puddle is built from the same helper, that writer may be resetting the keywords.
2. **The puddle albedo has no alpha channel.** `T_Ground_Puddle.png` is written as RGB by
   `scripts/puddle_build.py`, so it cannot carry smoothness in alpha the way the field and lane `_M` maps
   do -- the rule from item 3 does not transfer. The water's smoothness has to be a constant on the puddle
   material or a dedicated map. If you bound that albedo as `_MetallicGlossMap`, its missing alpha imports
   as the white default and every puddle is smoothness 1.0: check which of the two you actually did.
3. Fix it, then re-shoot the gameplay view and **measure the gradient**: sample the puddle from near edge
   to far edge and report brightness as a function of distance (and therefore of view angle). It must RISE
   toward the horizon. A flat line means the reflection is still ambient and the fix did not take.
4. **Also build the fallback in this same pass, so Todd has a real choice instead of an argument:** a
   second puddle material variant with a controlled, non-dielectric moon glint -- the standard game cheat,
   a shaped highlight in the water that reads at any angle. Frame both variants from the same camera, same
   puddle, same frame pair, and put both frames in CHIEF-STATUS.md. Keep the glint subtle: a hint of moon
   in water, not a mirror, and it lives on the puddle prefab so it is one switch to remove.
5. Then STOP and put it to Todd with the two frames. That is a look decision and he owns it.
