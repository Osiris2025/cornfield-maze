# CORN BRIEF — the field's building block

**For: Dough (@corn-art). Binding for the corn field work.** Facts below are measured, not estimated —
from the block set in `Assets/Corn/Blocks/`, its manifest, and the generator in `scripts/`. If something
here looks wrong, escalate to Harrow; do not adjust it locally.

## 1. What it is

The field is built from a **2.0 × 2.0 m corn block**: 9 plants on a 3 × 3 grid with jitter, the
**tight 0.9 m** leaf spread, heights 10 ft ± 5 %.

**Todd signed this off on 2026-09-24** — density and look. `Assets/Corn/Blocks/` holds **six distinct
blocks**, each with two LODs. **WE DO NOT RE-MODEL THE PLANT.** The two source models are third-party
and are used as authored (see §8).

## 2. What it replaces

The procedural per-cell scatter in `Assets/Scripts/MazeWorldBuilder.cs`:

```csharp
AddCornBlock(blockRoot.transform, center, maze.CellSize);
int stalks = 6 + rng.Next(4);            // 6-9 stalks in a 4 x 4 m cell
```

That is **0.4–0.6 plants/m²**. The block set is **2.25 plants/m²** — 4 blocks per 4 × 4 m wall cell =
36 plants, a **4–6× density increase**. The old `CornPlant.cs` primitives stay in the tree as the
fallback path until the block swap lands.

## 4. The sparseness is the design — do not "fix" it

Measured with 144 rays from the corridor eye position to a 1.45 m shape standing inside a 4 m thick wall:

| Density | Shape visible through the wall |
|---|---|
| **2.25 plants/m² (shipped)** | **5.6 %** |
| 4 plants/m² | 0.7 % |
| 9 plants/m² | 0.0 % |

Todd's requirement, verbatim: *"every once in a while, you might see the rustle and glance from some
boogeyman peeking at you through the corn."* At 9 plants/m² the wall is a solid green bank and nothing
will ever peek through it. The glimpse comes from the **base of the wall**, where the lower metre is
bare stems with ground behind it.

**Punching gaps in a dense block does not work** — neighbouring plants' leaves overhang ~0.9 m into the
hole and fill it (measured: 0.0 % visible). If a guaranteed peek spot is ever wanted, it must be a real
hole with no plants either side.

## 5. UNPROVEN — it must load and render correctly IN UNITY

Blender previews prove nothing about the Unity import. Two traps:

1. **Blend mode.** FBX carries no material blend mode. The leaf cards **must be set to Cutout with
   alpha clip 0.5**, or every leaf renders as a solid slab. This is the single most likely failure.
2. **Materials must be authored.** The FBX carries UVs and material slots only — no textures, no
   alpha, no normal map wiring. Textures are in `Assets/Corn/Textures/`.

Also expected and not a bug: the source textures are small (`T_Corn_01_D` 256 × 1024,
`corn_texture.png` 512 × 512), so the corn is **soft at arm's length**. Crisper corn means authoring a
new atlas — a separate decision, not a fix.

## 6. Materials

| Plants | Albedo | Normal | Alpha |
|---|---|---|---|
| corn-old (7 of 9) | `Textures/T_Corn_01_D.png` | `Textures/T_Corn_01_NRM.png` | opaque |
| corn-corn-corn (2 of 9) | `Textures/corn_texture.png` | — | **from the albedo's alpha → Cutout** |

One material per source model, instancing on. The block's mesh keeps the two material slots in the
order `corn` (corn-corn-corn) then `Maize` (corn-old) — slot names came from the source files and are
misleading; match on the texture, not the slot name.

## 7. Rebuilding the blocks

They are **generated by code, never hand-modelled** — that is why six blocks from six seeds look
different while sharing one look:

```
python3 scripts/corn_block_build.py        # design-time only; never in the build
```

Changing the leaf factor or the density changes **every** block, so the LODs and the manifest must be
regenerated in the same commit. Seeds are the recipe: block *k* uses seed `1661 + 1013 × k`.
