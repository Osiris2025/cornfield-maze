# Asset provenance — Corn Field Maze

Every asset that is not generated by our own code goes here, with what it replaces, why, and its licence.

## The gingerbread man — PROVIDED BY TODD

- **Received:** 2026-09-17, from Todd, as `gingerbread_man.zip`.
- **Files:** `gb_man.fbx`, `textures/2048x2048/gb_man_color.png`, `textures/2048x2048/gb_man_normals.png`, `textures/512x512/gb_man_color_512.png`, `textures/512x512/gb_man_normals_512.png`.
- **In-project:** `Assets/GingerbreadMan/gb_man.fbx` + `Assets/GingerbreadMan/textures/*_512.png`.
  **Full archive (including the 2048 maps):** `docs/reference/gingerbread_man/` — reference, deliberately outside `Assets/`, never imported.
- **What it replaces:** the procedural primitive cookie in `FarmWalkerController.cs` → `GingerbreadMesh.Build()` (~40 `CreatePrimitive` cubes and spheres). Todd: *"we are very unhappy with the gingerbread man."*
- **What it is, measured — first by parsing the FBX binary, then independently by importing it in Blender:**
  - **25 bones** (`LimbNode`), skinned via 84 `Deformer`/`Cluster` objects, 4 bind `Pose` nodes.
  - Bone names: `spine01`, `spine02`, `neck`, `head`, `chin.L`, `chin.R`, `shoulder.L/R`, `upper_arm.L/R`, `forearm.L/R`, `hand.L/R`, `hip.L/R`, `waist.L/R`, `foot.L/R`, plus `head_end`, `hand.L_end`, `hand.R_end`, `foot.L_end`, `foot.R_end`.
  - **4 meshes, split by ROLE**, totalling **1,796 verts / 3,238 triangles** (Blender's triangulation is authoritative; an earlier "~2,221" figure in this file was a bad estimate from raw polygon-index entries and is superseded):
    | mesh | verts | tris | what it is |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `gb_man_body` | 1010 | 2016 | the cookie |
    | **`gb_man_decoration`** | 466 | 742 | **the icing — its own mesh** |
    | `gb_man_eyes` | 192 | 288 | the ring eyes |
    | `gb_man_mouth` | 128 | 192 | the red ring mouth |
  - **1 material**, `gb_man_texture`, colour + normal map.
  - **No animation** — zero `AnimationStack` / `AnimationLayer` / `AnimCurveNode` in the file. The rig is static-posed; **the game must animate the joints in code**, which is what it already does for the primitive rig.
  - **Bounds: 5.376 units tall, 4.75 wide across the arms** (Blender import at scale 1). The player capsule is `height 1.80`, so the model needs **≈0.335 scale** or the capsule re-measured.
  - Previews rendered in Blender: `artifacts/review/cookie/ASSET-PREVIEW-{front,side,back,head}.png`.
- **Licence / provenance: UNKNOWN.** The file dates are 2020-12-11/14 and it carries no readme, no author string and no `Texture`/`Material` provenance node. Todd supplied it, so it is cleared for our own use, but **App Store shipping needs the source and licence confirmed**. Tracked here so it cannot quietly ship.

## The corn — SUPPLIED BY TODD, licence UNKNOWN

Three corn models were supplied on 2026-09-24 as `corn-old/`, `corn-corn-corn/` and `maize-corn-plant/`
(zips and loose folders under `~/Downloads`, outside the repo). **Two are used; all three are third-party
with no licence file, no author string and no readme.** Same status and the same shipping risk as the
gingerbread man.

| Source | What it is, measured | Used? |
|---|---|---|
| `corn-old/source/SM_Corn_01.fbx` | single upright stalk, **545 tris**, 2.254 m native, 21 objects — 20 separate leaf meshes + a separate stem mesh **46 × 44 mm** across. Textures `T_Corn_01_D.png` (256 × 1024) + `T_Corn_01_NRM.png`, dated 2023-11-07 | **yes — 7 of every 9 plants** |
| `corn-corn-corn/source/corn.fbx` | single upright plant, **2 570 tris** (1 410 verts / 1 259 faces), 3.855 m native, carries a 0.0838 node scale and needs a −90° X fix. **ASCII FBX 6.1** (Blender 2.70 exporter). Its texture path reads `RunZombieRun\Assets\Textures\corn_texture.png` (512 × 512, dated 2018-03-15) — so it came out of a project called **RunZombieRun** | **yes — 2 of every 9 plants** |
| `maize-corn-plant/source/model.zip` → `model/model.dae` | **not a stalk** — a 42-mesh clump, 3 108 tris, 73 × 69 × 77 units, full PBR sets (3 material variants incl. alpha) | **no** — a patch/clump asset, no use in the field |

- **In-project:** `Assets/Corn/Blocks/*.fbx` + `Assets/Corn/Textures/*.png` (5.0 MB). These are **our own
  generated assets**, derived from the two models above by code — six 2 × 2 m blocks plus LODs, and a
  manifest. The generator is `scripts/corn_block_build.py`; the design facts are in
  `docs/CORN-BRIEF.md`, the FSD section is §24.
- **Blender 5 cannot read either used model** (Collada importer removed; the FBX is ASCII 6.1 with
  per-polygon-vertex UVs). Conversion is by stdlib script, not by a tool — `scripts/corn_block_lib.py`
  documents the two traps it hit: UVs are indexed by `UVIndex` (not by vertex), and the Collada parent
  matrix had to be applied in column-major order.
- **Licence: UNKNOWN for both used models.** `SM_Corn_01`/`T_Corn_01` is Unreal-marketplace naming and
  the other came from someone else's game project. **App Store shipping needs the source and licence
  confirmed** — raise it with Todd before submission, do not let it ship silently.
