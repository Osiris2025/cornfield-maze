WHAT THIS PASS FIXED

1. THE PUDDLES WERE LAID ACROSS THE LANE. `PuddleDecals` rotates each decal into the lane's axis, and the
   mesh puts its LONG side on local X. The rotation said "east-west lane -> 90 deg", which turned the long
   side onto Z: every east-west puddle lay ACROSS the lane — 3.4 m of water across a 2.08 m lane, running
   into the corn on both sides. Now east-west gets 0 and north-south gets 90, so the long side follows the
   lane in both cases. This is the defect the first pass shipped and did not see.

2. THE "MISSING NORMAL MAP" WAS MY READ-BACK'S FAULT, NOT THE MATERIAL'S. The first report said the built
   app reads the puddle's normal map as "none". It asked for `_NormalMap`; URP/Lit's property is `_BumpMap`.
   The map was never missing. The read-back now asks for the property the shader actually has, and it reads:
   baseMap=T_Ground_PuddleA normalMap=T_Ground_Puddle_N metallicGlossMap=T_Ground_Puddle_M
   smoothnessChannel=0 _Smoothness=1.00 keyword _METALLICSPECGLOSSMAP=True. Nothing is unbound.

3. THE MIRROR DISTANCE IS NOW DERIVED FROM THE MOON, NOT GUESSED. At an eye height of 1.655 m and the moon
   at 28 deg the reflection lands 3.12 m from the water's centre — that is arithmetic, not a choice. The
   harness computes it from the live moon direction instead of the 2.5 m it used before.

4. THE INSTRUMENT NOW MEASURES THE PICTURE. Each frame projects the decal's own quad and counts bright
   pixels (G > 140, i.e. over 0.55 of full) INSIDE the waterline and in the rest of the band, so "the moon is
   caught in a puddle" and "the lane round it stays matte" stop being eyeball claims.

WHAT IS STILL BROKEN, PLAINLY

- The frame is still not the acceptance frame. The harness keeps standing the player off the lane. It now
  probes four candidate directions with a downward ray and takes the one whose surface is the lane material;
  the probe reports "a surface whose base map is no base map", so the maze's colliders are not the renderers
  carrying the lane's `_BaseMap` and the test never matches. It falls back to a computed spot, which is in
  corn. Next pass: ask the MAZE for the lane cell (MazeData already knows which cells are path) instead of
  raycasting the world for a material.
- The projected decal rect is still unreliable: corners near the camera plane project to absurd coordinates
  (this pass: -5975 px) and poison the min/max. It needs a clamp and a rejection distance, not just a
  z > 0 test.
- The band's numbers this pass, for the record: with the reflective maps bound the band's mean G is 40.74 of
  255 with a peak of 255; with them unbound it is 22.57 with a peak of 120. That 1.8x and that peak are the
  GROUND's own highlight, not the puddle's — the lane is what Todd called a bug — and it is exactly why the
  puddle's contribution has to be isolated by the projected rect before anything is claimed. I am not
  claiming the puddle is visible in this frame, because it is not.
