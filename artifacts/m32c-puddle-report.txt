It was wrong on the first build, and the read-back caught it in one run:

    array +Y mean G -560.08, post-upload 0.00, g range -2.203 .. -0.769

`Mathf.SmoothStep(from, to, t)` clamps t and returns a value BETWEEN from AND to — it is not "smooth a 0..1 ramp".

to black. And a black probe cubemap does not merely fail to light the water: it took the ground's ambient with
it (field 22.32 -> 6.45, water 46.56 -> 5.90). Normalised (`r = ang / 3.6`, smooth 1 -> 0 across the disc) and
