WHY THE PUDDLE WAS NEVER IN SHOT — NOW MEASURED, NOT GUESSED

Pass 4 replaced the corner-projection counter with the measurement the milestone actually asks for: every
second pixel of the frame is classified by geometry — cast the camera's own ray at the ground plane and ask
whether that world point falls inside the decal's quad. The first run of that instrument answered the whole
question in one line: the puddle was **0 px of the frame**.

The cause: the moon's reflection lies along the MOON's azimuth, not along the lane (off a flat plane the
reflection's horizontal direction is the moon's). Passes 1-3 stood the player up-lane and then aimed at the
mirror point, so the mirror point landed on bare lane somewhere and the puddle sat out of shot the whole
time. The fix is to choose the stand on the moon's own azimuth and snap it to the nearest path cell, so the
stand is still on the lane by the maze's own answer.

chasing something that is not there: the lobe is tight and the moon's disc is small, so the honest physical
answer may be a small glint that this lighting does not deliver. The more valuable finding is the wash on the
lane, because that is what Todd objected to by eye, and it is measurable.
