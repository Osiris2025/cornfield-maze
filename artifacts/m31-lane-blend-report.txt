M31 — the lane blends by transparency, not geometry
Harrow @corn-chief, for Todd. Corn Field Maze, Unity 6000.3.23f1, Mac build / iPhone target.

Todd, 2026-09-25: "the path way should not be layered, it should be blended via transparency. the
path is currently bubbly and janky." He was looking at a preview; the built game had the same
defect, and M29's own report named the cause in one line: "Blend method: geometry."

What changed: the lane's margin is now an alpha fade, not a mesh. M29 pulled the lane's boundary
vertices inwards on a derived mask, so the join was a cut line with polygon facets — a margin
that wanders 0.42 m is still a cut. The lane is now a plain rectangle laid over the field and the
whole boundary is the alpha strip, reaching zero exactly at the mesh's edge.

The fade is baked into the lane albedo's alpha at DESIGN time (scripts/m31_lane_alpha_bake.py,
Blender), so the material stays stock URP/Lit and the lane keeps the moon, its normal map and the
PathMudWetness path. Baking also deletes the CPU-readable mask copy M29 needed — the phone cost
that milestone asked to remove.

5. WHAT I AM NOT CLAIMING
  - At a corner the cell's core piece fades the axis the lane runs along, and its OTHER corn-facing
    edge keeps a hard line. A linear strip cannot fade two adjacent sides, and the alternative — two
    overlapping pieces — was measured as a brighter plate with a straight edge, which is the exact
    defect this milestone removes. The corner frame shows which edge is left hard. Fixing it needs a
    second fade direction, not a bigger number.
  - The A/B of blend cost was run by switching the live lane material between Alpha Blend and Opaque
    over the same view. It reads as nothing measurable on the Mac. That is an honest reading of a
    machine that is not fill-bound at 3 ms/frame, not a claim that overdraw is free on a phone.
  - A flat magenta tint on the lane material did NOT reach the drawn pixels in the coverage frame
    (0 magenta pixels, checked numerically). So the lane's runtime colour is driven from somewhere
    other than this material's _BaseColor, and the plan view is reported without the tint rather
    than pretending it worked. The coverage COUNT below does not depend on the tint.
  - The bright quadrilateral on the ground in the night lane frame is present in M29's committed
    frame of the same view, unchanged: it is not a regression from this pass and not the lane's
    boundary. It is unidentified and left alone.
  - Do not change: the lane's navigable footprint (M22's lane-centring, the corridor constraint),
    the eat sequence, the HUD, the maze layout and M26's threat term were not touched.

Verdict: at the lane's edge the gravel dissolves into the field with no cut line, no facets and no
visible edge where the layer stops — compare m29-ground-edge.png against m31-ground-edge.png. Open:
the corner's remaining hard edge, and the phone-side overdraw that the Mac cannot measure.
