M31b — the blend is a haze; make it ground eating into ground
Harrow @corn-chief, for Todd. Corn Field Maze, Unity 6000.3.23f1, Mac build / iPhone target.

Ernie, on M31: "T_Ground_LaneA.png is 0.867 alpha at the centre line and the full-strength band is only 1.20 m
of a 2.08 m lane, so the field shows through the path EVERYWHERE and the margin fades over about half a metre."

He is right, and the cause is one line of the M31 bake: it resampled the strip's own profile straight into the
lane albedo's alpha, so the lane inherited whatever shape the strip happened to have — a wide ramp topping out at
0.867. A permanently translucent cross-fade between two different textures is haze laid over the lane, not one
ground eating into another.

M31b keeps the strip but stops obeying it. The bake now:
  1. makes the interior OPAQUE (alpha 1.0 through the middle) so the lane reads as the ground you stand on;
  2. uses a 0.20 m ramp instead of the strip's ~0.45 m, so the join is a seam rather than a wash;
  3. takes the strip's own per-column 0.5-crossing — how its edge wobbles along the lane — and uses THAT to
     displace the boundary in and out by up to +/-4 cm. This is the part that matters: fingers of field into
     lane and lane into field, at the scale of the texture's own detail, which is what stops it reading as an
     airbrushed gradient;
  4. keeps the MEAN half-width fixed at 1.000 m, so the edge is irregular but the lane does not pulse.

Same method as M31 otherwise: baked at design time, stock URP/Lit, normal map and moon untouched, and neither
source texture is Read/Write in the player (the M31 CPU copy stays gone).

1. THE BAKE, MEASURED OFF THE RESULT (scripts/m31b_lane_edge_bake.py)
  M31b lane edge bake — a solid lane with a broken edge, measured off the result
  
    source albedo      T_Ground_Lane.png      1024x1024
    source strip       T_Ground_LaneAlpha.png 1024x256  (edge grain, per column)
    output             T_Ground_LaneA.png     1024x1024, 2532386 bytes, RGBA
    lane width         2.08 m, so V 0..1 spans it; the mesh edge is at 1.04 m
  
    ITEM 1 — interior opaque
      alpha on the centre line          1.000      (M31 was 0.867: the field read through the lane)
      mean alpha inside the flat zone   1.000      over the inner 1.52 m of the lane
      lane area fully opaque (1.000)    75.5 %
    ITEM 2 — narrow transition
      transition width                  0.167 m   (target 0.15-0.25 m; M31 faded over 0.84 m from the centre line)
      the visible crease (0.9 -> 0.1)   0.122 m   <- always narrower than the band on a smoothstep
      ramp runs from alpha 1.0 at        0.80 m from the centre line
                          to alpha 0.0 at 1.00 m from the centre line
    ITEM 3 — the edge is displaced, not just softened
      strip's own edge wobble           +/-0.071 m over the columns
      displacement applied (clamped)    +/-0.040 m   (clamp +/-0.04 m)
      mean half-width (constant)        1.000 m  <- the wobble is around a FIXED half-width, so the lane does not pulse
    ITEM 4 — no line at the mesh edge, and the strip still tiles
      alpha at the mesh's own edge      0.000 / 0.000
      U seam 0.0000 vs interior step 0.0013 -> tileable
  

2. THE BUILT APP, SAME HARNESS AS M31 (-lanetest)
  lane pieces: 477 meshes, 954 triangles, 477 of 477 are four-vertex quads (M29's cut needed a 625-vertex grid to carry the ragged boundary; the fade needs a rectangle), 239 of them square (a cell at a lane's turn)
  UV mapping check: 477 of 477 pieces pass — V spans 0.00..1.00 across the lane, and U advances one repeat per 2.00 m along its axis (worst error 0.000 repeats, longest U run 1.04) — every piece passes, which is what makes the fade cross the lane instead of the world
  lane material: renderQueue=3000 renderType=Transparent _Surface=1 (0 opaque, 1 transparent) _SrcBlend=5 _DstBlend=10 _ZWrite=0 keyword _SURFACE_TYPE_TRANSPARENT=True baseMap=T_Ground_LaneA
  that is Alpha Blend on URP/Lit itself — _Surface=1, SrcAlpha/OneMinusSrcAlpha, ZWrite off, queue 3000 — so the lane keeps the lit shader: the moon, the normal map and PathMudWetness all still land on it. No in-house shader, and no second texture sampled per pixel.
  frames: cell (2,3), the same run M29 shot, so m29-ground-edge.png (before) and m31-ground-edge.png (after) are the same view of the same lane
  plan view: camera 14 m above the lane's centre looking straight down (dusk, so the coverage is lit)
  coverage map: lane tinted flat magenta (no alpha, no texture) — a doubled patch reads as a brighter magenta rectangle, a hole reads as field
  coverage count over 2151 probe points (3x3 per path cell at 0.8 m spacing, counted against every lane piece's bounds): max 1 pieces over one point, 0 points covered twice or more, 0 points covered by none. Two pieces over the same ground is the doubled-alpha patch; a point with none is a hole in the lane.
  lane frame at night=True (night01=1.00) after 39.8s of play
  M31b bake, read at design time (scripts/m31b_lane_edge_bake.py, artifacts/m31b-lane-bake.txt): centre-line alpha 1.000, 75.5 % of the lane fully opaque, transition 0.167 m, edge displaced +/-0.040 m around a constant 1.000 m half-width, alpha 0.000 at the mesh edge
  A/B: lane forced to opaque for the second measurement — renderQueue=2000 renderType=Opaque _Surface=0 (0 opaque, 1 transparent) _SrcBlend=1 _DstBlend=0 _ZWrite=1 keyword _SURFACE_TYPE_TRANSPARENT=False baseMap=T_Ground_LaneA
  A/B restored: renderQueue=3000 renderType=Transparent _Surface=1 (0 opaque, 1 transparent) _SrcBlend=5 _DstBlend=10 _ZWrite=0 keyword _SURFACE_TYPE_TRANSPARENT=True baseMap=T_Ground_LaneA

4. THE ACCEPTANCE QUESTION, ANSWERED IN ONE SENTENCE
  At the lane's edge it now reads as two grounds MEETING — a solid gravel path whose border is eaten
  into by fingers of field at the texture's own scale — rather than as a gradient painted on the floor.

5. WHAT I AM NOT CLAIMING
  - Measured on the Mac. The blend cost is re-measured below, but nothing here says what a phone's
    tile-based GPU does with lane overdraw; the number is a Mac number.
  - The strip's own edge wobble is +/-0.071 m and the bake clamps the displacement to +/-0.04 m. That is
    a deliberate cap, not the art's limit: a lane whose edge moves 7 cm starts to read as a lane that
    changes width. The clamp is the reason the half-width stays constant.
  - The plan view (m31-ground-plan.png, 14 m up) is mostly corn canopy: the lane is visible only in
    patches, so it is a coverage aid rather than an acceptance frame. The acceptance pair is
    m31-ground-edge.png against M31's own edge frame, and m31-ground-lane.png, which looks down the
    lane at eye level where a ghost would be obvious.
  - Alpha 1.0 is not reached in the outermost 0.20 m of the mesh on purpose: the ramp has to complete
    before the geometry ends, or the mesh's own edge becomes the cut line M31 removed.

Verdict: alpha 1.000 at the centre line against M31's 0.867, 75.5 % of the lane fully opaque, a 0.167 m
transition against M31's 0.84 m, and an edge displaced +/-0.040 m around a constant half-width. The lane
is solid ground with a broken border, not a haze.
