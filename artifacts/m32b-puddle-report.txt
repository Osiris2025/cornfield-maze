M32b — puddles: the system is built and the sheen is verified; the ACCEPTANCE FRAME is not yet obtained

Harrow @corn-chief, for Todd. Corn Field Maze, Unity 6000.3.23f1, Mac build / iPhone target.
2026-09-25. Status: OPEN. Read this before treating M32b as done.

WHAT IS BUILT AND WHAT IS PROVEN

The pieces Ernie shipped are wired and the sheen lives in the right place. Two derived maps were added at
design time by scripts/m32b_puddle_maps.py, because the material could not do its job without them:

  T_Ground_PuddleA.png   RGB = the wet earth, A = the coverage (the material is Alpha Blend and URP reads the
                         base map's alpha as opacity — the coverage file keeps it in RGB, so it has to be
                         moved into the alpha, exactly as M31's lane fade does)
  T_Ground_Puddle_M.png  RGB metallic 0, A = smoothness: ~0.88 in the water, ~0.10 across the damp halo

Measured off the result (artifacts/m32b-puddle-bake.txt):

  water core (coverage > 0.70)          7.6 % of the quad
  damp halo                             21.1 % of the quad
  max coverage                          0.86 — never 1.0, so the lane's grain reads through the water
  smoothness inside the water           mean 0.875, max 0.880     (target 0.85-0.92)
  smoothness in the damp halo           mean 0.104                (MATTE — the highlight stops at the waterline)

And the order's acceptance, SAMPLED out of the imported texture in the built game rather than eyeballed
(artifacts/m32b-puddle-report.txt, line 13):

  puddle read-back: T_Ground_Puddle_M 512x512 smoothness max 0.902, mean inside the water (alpha > 0.7)
  0.876 over 7.6% of the quad, 92.1% of the quad below 0.2 (the damp halo and the dry edge) -> WATER REFLECTS

  lane read-back:   T_Ground_Lane_M alpha mean 0.137, 0.0% above 0.40 -> MATCH

Puddle smoothness > 0.7: yes (0.902 max, 0.876 mean in the water). Lane still < 0.2: yes (0.137). The half of
the acceptance that can be sampled is green.

Placement, per the order's rule — decals in lane low spots, a handful per level, elongated along the lane,
never tiled, never on the field:

  6 puddles, placed by PuddleDecals.cs on cells that are on a STRAIGHT run (exactly two neighbours, opposite
  each other) out of 180 candidates, stride 30 so they spread over the maze. Each is one quad, 2.6 x 1.5 m,
  rotated into the lane's axis with up to 8 deg of yaw, lifted 6 mm above the lane to avoid z-fighting. The
  short side (1.5 m) is inside the 2.08 m lane, so a puddle cannot reach the corn. Everything is under ONE
  parent named "Puddles" — removing every puddle is deleting one object, and nothing puddle-shaped is baked
  into the tiled ground textures.

WHY IT IS NOT GREEN: THE FRAME

The acceptance is "a night frame with the moon caught in a puddle while the lane round it stays matte". I have
the second half of that sentence in frames and the first half only in numbers. The shot
(artifacts/review/world/m32b-puddle-moon.png) stands the player 2.58 m up-lane from the measured puddle and
aims at the solved mirror point, which lands 0.53 m past the puddle's centre and therefore inside a decal that
is 2.6 m long — the geometry is right. The frame still does not show a legible moon in the water: the puddle
reads as a slightly darker patch on an already dark lane, and I could not point at the highlight in the
picture. The band's peak of 255 in that frame is not evidence — a bright pixel in a 130x130 window is not the
same thing as the moon in a puddle, and I am not going to call it one.

What I would change next pass, in order:
  1. the decal is nearly invisible at night because the water's albedo (mean 0.114) sits close to the night
     lane's value once the moon is the only light — the contrast between wet earth and dry lane needs to be
     raised, or the water made slightly more reflective across a wider area;
  2. the aim should be verified by projecting the PUDDLE's own bounds into the frame and reporting the pixels
     it covers, not just the mirror point — "the band is at the mirror point" is not "the puddle is in shot";
  3. only then re-shoot, and put the frame beside m32b-mirror-contrast.png, where the lane's own defect is put
     back for comparison.

WHAT ELSE THIS PASS MEASURED, FOR THE RECORD

  * The puddle material is Alpha Blend on URP/Lit itself (renderQueue 3000, SrcAlpha/OneMinusSrcAlpha, ZWrite
    off) with the metallic/smoothness keyword ON and `_SmoothnessTextureChannel` 0, so the water's reflectivity
    reaches the shader the same way the ground's matte does. The normal map reads "none" in the built app's
    read-back while the albedo and the gloss map both resolve — worth a look next pass; it does not affect the
    smoothness result, but a flat water plane without its normal is not what was designed.
  * The harness re-ran the whole M32 ground measurement on the way through, and it still holds: lane 0.137,
    field 0.095, both MATCH, 0.0 % above 0.40, and the frame subtraction still shows the reflective and matte
    ground states are the same picture.
  * One bug worth recording because it cost two runs: the M32b harness was a copy of M32's, and I renamed the
    class but not the component its installer created — so `-puddletest` launched the M32 harness and quietly
    wrote the M32 report. It presented as "the harness never writes a report". A copy is not a rename.

m32b-reflection-off.png, m32b-field-only.png. Raw harness output: artifacts/m32b-puddle-report.txt.

Verdict: the puddle system is built, placed and verified by read-back — water 0.902, lane 0.137 — but the
acceptance frame is not obtained, so M32b is OPEN, not green. Nothing else ships on this claim.
